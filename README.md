## About me

I work primarily with the SAP stack, including SAP UI5, Fiori, ABAP, RAP, BTP and EWM, alongside modern web technologies such as TypeScript and cloud-based architectures. My role spans project implementation and product development, shaping SAP solutions that fit real business needs.

I enjoy sharing knowledge: mentoring trainees, running training sessions, discussing best practices with colleagues and speaking at community events such as ABAPConf and UI5ers Live.

I love solving complex problems and discovering new technologies along the way. Beyond SAP, I like to take things apart and understand how they work, whether that's an unfamiliar framework, automation, AI tooling or an app of my own. That curiosity usually finds its way back into my daily work as cleaner code, better tooling and more pragmatic solutions. Away from the keyboard you'll find me DJing or traveling.
